The sitemap is rebuilt automatically every time a new article is published, which currently happens at least once per parliamentary working day.

Is there a machine-readable version?

Every article is generated in 14 languages, but for very recent items the pipeline publishes English first and other languages within a few minutes. Refresh the page if a translation appears to be missing.

Can I link to a specific category?

Yes. Each category heading on this page has a stable id you can deep-link to. The sitemap.xml file additionally exposes every article URL with a lastmod timestamp for crawlers.
