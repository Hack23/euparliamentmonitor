## KEY DECISIONS

### 1. DMA Big Tech Enforcement (TA-10-2026-0160) 🟢 HIGH PRIORITY
Parliament's resolution demands the Commission escalate DMA enforcement against Apple, Google, Meta, Amazon, and Microsoft. Specific demands: market investigation for App Store interoperability by Q3 2026; maximum fines for repeated infringements; delineation of new gatekeepers.

**Intelligence assessment:** Enforcement will proceed but face 2–4 year legal delay through litigation. DMA's economic potential — if enforced — is estimated at €75–150B in EU market efficiency gains over 5 years (IMF methodology). The US-EU diplomatic dimension is the primary risk variable.

**Stakeholders most affected:** Apple (App Store revenue model), Google (Search bundling), Commission DG COMP (enforcement capacity), EU app developers (~500,000 SMEs)

### 2. Ukraine Accountability Framework (TA-10-2026-0161) 🟢 HIGH PRIORITY
EP established accountability task force with mandate covering: documentation of atrocities, ICC cooperation, asset seizure mechanisms for reparations, and post-conflict justice architecture.

**Intelligence assessment:** The framework has strong EP legitimacy but requires Council agreement for most implementation steps. Hungary and potentially Italy represent Council-level blocking risks. Russian disinformation operations are actively targeting this agenda.

**Stakeholders most affected:** Ukrainian government (reparations architecture), ICC (cooperation mandate), Russia (sanctions compliance pressure), Hungary (blocking role at risk of isolation)

### 3. Budget 2027 Guidelines (TA-10-2026-0112) 🟡 MEDIUM PRIORITY
EP guidelines prioritise: 5% defence spending increase, full Horizon Europe funding protection, CAP modernisation, and demand for new own resources including digital levy.

**Intelligence assessment:** Council will resist digital levy (requires unanimity) and some defence increases. Expected outcome: 2–3% defence increase; Horizon Europe protected; digital levy deferred to 2028 review. IMF context: euro area fiscal space constrained with France at 5.1% deficit, Italy at 4.8%.

**Stakeholders most affected:** Member state finance ministries (contribution increases), defence contractors (spending uplift), research community (Horizon protection), Commission DG BUDG

### 4. Livestock Sustainability (TA-10-2026-0157) 🟡 MEDIUM PRIORITY
Resolution sets 2028 targets for cage-free systems, methane reduction, and improved transport standards. Technology-neutral language accommodates both intensive and organic producers.

**Intelligence assessment:** The resolution reflects EP9's failed Farm-to-Fork lesson — incremental, producer-friendly framing maintains coalition. Implementation will be member-state level; enforcement gap risk high. EU livestock sector: €168B GDP exposure.

### 5. Cyberbullying Criminal Law (TA-10-2026-0163) 🟡 MEDIUM PRIORITY
Resolution calls for EU-level criminal law harmonisation for online harassment, coordinated with DSA enforcement. Youth safety framing has broad public support.

**Intelligence assessment:** Council requires unanimity on criminal law; probable route is enhanced cooperation or DSA administrative remedy rather than Treaty-based criminal competence expansion.
