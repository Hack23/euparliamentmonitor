# Historical Parallels — EP10 Term Outlook (to June 2029)

> Draws on prior EP terms and analogous episodes to contextualise EP10's arc.
> Admiralty source grades. Confidence 🟢/🟡/🔴.

## 1. Parallel Frame

- Uses EP6–EP9 patterns as the primary historical reference.
- Identifies what recurs and what is genuinely new in EP10.

## 2. Recurring Patterns

- **Election-year output dip.** Observed across prior terms. Source B2. 🟢 HIGH.
- **Mid-term productivity peak.** Years 3–4 are most productive. Source B2. 🟢 HIGH.
- **Rising fragmentation each term.** ENP trend up since 2004. Source A2. 🟢 HIGH.

## 3. The Genuine Break

- **Loss of the grand coalition.** First directly elected EP where EPP+S&D < 361.
  Source A2. 🟢 HIGH.
- **A pivotal hard-right bloc.** New swing capacity on the right. Source C3. 🟡 MEDIUM.

## 5. Lessons from Prior Terms

- Output arcs are reliable; coalition arrangements are not. Source B3. 🟡 MEDIUM.
- A strong pivot can sustain delivery despite fragmentation. Source C3. 🟡 MEDIUM.
- Firewall norms have historically held on institutional votes. Source C3. 🟡 MEDIUM.

## 6. Where Parallels Break Down

- No prior term combined this fragmentation with no grand coalition.
  Source C4. 🟡 MEDIUM.
- The right-bloc swing capacity has no clean EP precedent. Source C4. 🟡 MEDIUM.

## 7. Reader Briefing

- EP10's output arc rhymes with history; its coalition map does not.
- The structural break is the absent grand coalition.
- Treat coalition forecasts with more humility than output forecasts.
