# Media Framing Analysis — EP10 Term Outlook (to June 2029)

> How the remaining EP10 mandate is likely to be framed across media ecosystems.
> Confidence 🟢/🟡/🔴. Source grades A1–F6.

## 1. Purpose

- Anticipate the dominant narrative frames around EP10's term.
- Distinguish institutional, partisan, and adversarial framings.

## 2. Dominant Frames

- **"Fragmented parliament" frame.** Emphasises the loss of the grand coalition.
  Likely dominant in centrist outlets. Source C3. 🟡 MEDIUM.
- **"Rightward shift" frame.** Emphasises hard-right gains and right-track votes.
  Source C3. 🟡 MEDIUM.
- **"Delivery vs gridlock" frame.** Emphasises output capacity. Source C4. 🟡 MEDIUM.
- **"Competitiveness turn" frame.** Emphasises industrial-policy pivot.
  Source C4. 🟡 MEDIUM.

## 3. Frame-by-Audience

- Centrist/pro-EU media: fragmentation + delivery frames.
- Conservative media: competitiveness + sovereignty frames.
- Right-populist media: rightward-shift + migration frames.
- Green/left media: climate-erosion + democratic-backsliding frames.

## 4. Framing Risks

- Over-simplification of variable geometry as "EPP lurches right." 🟡 MEDIUM.
- Conflation of the cordon's erosion with its collapse. 🟡 MEDIUM.
- Election-year amplification of every coalition wobble. 🟢 HIGH.

## 5. Counter-Framing (analytic neutrality)

- The data supports "variable geometry," not a single permanent realignment.
- The cordon is contested but has not broken on institutional votes (yet).
- Output is projected to peak then dip — a structural, not partisan, pattern.

## 6. Narrative Tripwires

- Any EPP-PfE institutional vote → "cordon broken" headlines (watch).
- 2027 output below model → "gridlock" framing surge.
- Migration surge → "rightward shift" framing surge.

## 7. Framing Timeline

- 2026–2027: delivery vs gridlock dominant.
- 2028: competitiveness + pre-campaign framing.
- 2029: full election framing; coalition narratives peak.

## 8. Reader Briefing

- Expect "fragmentation" and "rightward shift" to dominate coverage.
- The neutral read is variable geometry with an intact-but-contested cordon.
- Election-year framing will amplify minor coalition signals.
