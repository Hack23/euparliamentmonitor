# Forward Indicators — EP10 Term Outlook (to June 2029)

> The signpost catalogue: observable indicators that confirm or falsify the
> central projection. WEP bands + Admiralty grades. Confidence 🟢/🟡/🔴.

## 1. Indicator Philosophy

- Each indicator pairs an observable with a directional reading.
- Movement across a tripwire re-grades the relevant forecast.

## 2. Output Indicators

- Annual legislative-act count vs model (2027 ~120). Source A2. 🟢 HIGH.
- Plenary session count vs model. Source A2. 🟢 HIGH.
- Committee throughput / trilogue completion rate. Source B3. 🟡 MEDIUM.
- Reading: on-track if within ±10% of model. Likely.

## 3. Coalition Indicators

- Frequency of right-track votes on migration/agriculture. Source C3. 🟡 MEDIUM.
- Any cordon-crossing institutional vote. Source C3. 🟡 MEDIUM.
- Roll-call cohesion scores per group. Source B3. 🟡 MEDIUM.
- Reading: normalisation risk rises if right-track recurs. Likely.

## 4. Fragmentation Indicators

- ENP recomputed per national cycle. Source B2. 🟡 MEDIUM.
- Group-switching events. Source C3. 🟡 MEDIUM.
- Reading: ENP staying 6.3–6.8 = baseline. Likely.

## 5. Electoral Indicators

- National polling in the five largest delegations. Source C3. 🔴 LOW.
- Turnout signals from national/regional contests. Source C4. 🔴 LOW.
- Reading: directional only at this horizon. Roughly Even.

## 6. Indicator Scoreboard

| Indicator | Tripwire | WEP | Source |
|-----------|----------|-----|--------|
| 2027 acts | <100 = undershoot | Likely | A2 |
| Right-track votes | recurring = normalise | Likely | C3 |
| Cordon vote | any = regime change | Unlikely | C3 |
| ENP | >7.0 = drift | Unlikely | B2 |

## 7. Review Cadence

- Quarterly: output + coalition indicators.
- Annually: fragmentation + electoral indicators.
- Ad hoc: cordon-crossing tripwire (immediate).

## 8. Reader Briefing

- The output indicators are the most reliable confirmation signals.
- The cordon-crossing vote is the single highest-value tripwire.
- Electoral indicators are weak at three years out — directional only.
