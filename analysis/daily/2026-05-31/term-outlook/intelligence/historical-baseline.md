# Historical Baseline — EP10 Term Outlook (to June 2029)

> Establishes the historical reference path against which EP10 forward outlook
> is measured. Confidence: 🟢/🟡/🔴. Source grades A1–F6.

## 1. Purpose

- Anchor the term-outlook projection in observed EP6–EP10 patterns.
- Provide the comparison baseline for output, fragmentation, and coalitions.

## 2. Fragmentation Trajectory

- ENP rose from ~4.12 (2004, EP6) to 6.59 (2026, EP10). Source A2. 🟢 HIGH.
- Each term since EP7 added effective parties. 🟢 HIGH.
- EP10 is the most fragmented directly-elected chamber to date. 🟢 HIGH.

## 3. Coalition History

- EP6–EP9 sustained a workable EPP+S&D grand coalition (often + liberals).
- EP10 is the first term where EPP+S&D fall short of 361. 🟢 HIGH.
- Brokerage burden on EPP is historically unprecedented. 🟡 MEDIUM.

## 4. Output Baseline

- Historical terms show an election-year output dip. Source B2. 🟢 HIGH.
- Mid-term years (year 3–4) are the productivity peak. 🟢 HIGH.
- EP10 predictions: 2027 ~120 acts, 2028 ~125, 2029 dip ~78. 🟢 HIGH.

## 5. Baseline Comparison Chart

```mermaid
graph LR
  EP6[EP6 ENP 4.12] --> EP7[EP7]
  EP7 --> EP8[EP8]
  EP8 --> EP9[EP9 grand coalition]
  EP9 --> EP10[EP10 ENP 6.59 no grand coalition]
  EP10 --> EP11[EP11 2029 reset]
```

## 6. What Is Genuinely New in EP10

- Loss of the grand-coalition majority. 🟢 HIGH.
- A hard-right bloc large enough to be a swing factor. 🟡 MEDIUM.
- Variable-geometry voting as the modal pattern. 🟡 MEDIUM.

## 7. Baseline Indicators

- ENP per term (trend up).
- Grand-coalition seat share (trend down, now below majority).
- Election-year output ratio vs peak year.
- Number of effective veto players.

## 8. Reader Briefing

- EP10 continues the long fragmentation trend but crosses a threshold:
  the grand coalition no longer governs.
- The output arc (peak then election dip) is historically reliable.
