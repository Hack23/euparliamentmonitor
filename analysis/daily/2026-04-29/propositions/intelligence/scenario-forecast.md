# Scenario Forecast — EU Parliament Propositions
**Date:** 2026-04-29 | **Session:** Strasbourg April 28–29, 2026

## Scenario 1 — MFF 2028-2034 "Defence-Led Compromise" (55% probability)

### Description
The most likely outcome is a negotiated MFF compromise in which EP and Council converge on a ceiling of approximately €1.05-1.08 trillion (2025 prices), with a separate "defence envelope" of €80-100 billion partially financed through new own resources (e.g., a European defence levy or repurposed Cohesion Fund contributions from member states with large surpluses).

### Key Conditions
- Germany begins economic recovery in H2 2026 (aligned with IMF WEO April 2026 baseline +0.8% for 2026), reducing net-contributor fiscal resistance
- French EPP and German EPP negotiate a "grand bargain": defence uplift + modest cohesion cut + higher technology/strategic autonomy heading
- S&D secures written guarantee on ESF+ floor (minimum €15B/year) and climate tagging (27.5%+ compromise from EP's 30% demand)

### Timeline Projection
| Milestone | Estimated Date |
|-----------|---------------|
| Commission MFF proposal published | September 2026 |
| BUDG committee vote on EP position | January 2027 |
| EP first reading vote on MFF regulation | March-April 2027 |
| Council/EP MFF trilogue begins | May 2027 |
| Political agreement | October-December 2027 |
| Entry into force | January 2028 |

### Implications for Current Procedures
- The April 28 MFF interim report and budget guidelines set the opening position. The more aligned these are with a plausible compromise, the more negotiating leverage EP retains.
- Procedures for new instruments (ReArm Europe, EDIP) will be launched in parallel with MFF negotiations; their final form depends on MFF outcome.

**Confidence:** 🟡 Medium — contingent on German economic recovery and EP group discipline

## Scenario 2 — Trade War Escalation Stalls Legislation (20% probability)

### Description
A deterioration in the US-EU trade dispute triggers retaliatory tariff cycles that dominate the political agenda, delaying the 2025/0261 trilogue resolution and consuming bandwidth in INTA, ECON, and BUDG committees. MFF negotiations are delayed as member states prioritise trade crisis management.

### Key Conditions
- US announces 25% tariffs on EU automotive products (Volkswagen, BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Stellantis)
- EU invokes Regulation 2023/2655 (countermeasures mechanism) → retaliatory tariffs on $25-30B in US goods
- US responds with escalation → tit-for-tat cycle emerges

### Economic Impact
- **IMF WEO April 2026 projection in this scenario:** Germany GDP declines 0.8-1.2% in 2027 (third year of contraction)
- EU export sectors (automotive, machinery, chemicals, luxury goods) lose an estimated €40-60B in annual US revenues
- EU unemployment rises by ~0.4 percentage points to ~6.2%

### Legislative Impact
- INTA trilogue on 2025/0261 breaks down; procedure referred back to committee
- GSP reform implementation delays as trade policy bandwidth consumed
- MFF negotiations pause as EU budget contributions fall with declining economic activity
- Animal welfare and social legislation unaffected (domestic domain)

**Confidence:** 🟠 HIGH probability IF US automotive tariffs materialise; LOW baseline probability absent that trigger

## Scenario 3 — Coalition Fracture Delays Social Agenda (15% probability)

### Description
The consent-based rape legislation momentum built in the April 28 resolution stalls as EPP conservative wing (Poland, Italy, Croatia, Slovakia delegations) refuses to support any binding EU minimum standards legislation. The Commission declines to bring a new legislative proposal. The S&D/Greens/Left alliance claims political victory (non-binding resolution adopted) but fails to advance binding EU law.

### Key Conditions
- New German coalition (CDU/CSU + SPD formed February 2025) signals opposition to new social competence expansion at EU level
- Polish EPP delegation (largest EPP national delegation after German CSU/CDU) lobbies Weber to keep conservative social policy off the EU agenda
- Commission's work programme for H2 2026 excludes a new criminal law minimum standards directive

### Implications
- Non-binding resolutions like TA-10-2026-0120 deliver symbolic victories without legislative substance
- S&D faces electoral accountability deficit if consent legislation fails to advance in EP10
- Greens/EFA and Left may pursue enhanced cooperation mechanism (9+ member states) to create legal precedent

**Confidence:** 🟡 Medium — EPP conservative veto on social legislation is well-documented historical pattern; this scenario is primarily about whether the trend continues in EP10

## Scenario 4 — Pipeline Health Restoration (10% probability)

### Description
A procedural and technical breakthrough in the EP's legislative pipeline sees the backlog of stalled procedures (currently showing 0 enriched active procedures per `monitor_legislative_pipeline` API) resolved. This is partly a data quality issue (EP API enrichment delay) and partly a genuine procedural backlog.

### Key Conditions
- EP Secretariat completes the digital modernisation of the Legislative Observatory (OEIL) to support real-time procedure tracking
- BUDG, INTA, and ECON committees clear the backlog of procedures awaiting committee reports
- EP-Council relations improve post-MFF agreement

### Implications
- Improved legislative throughput would allow EP to demonstrate legislative ambition to citizens ahead of EP11 (2029 elections)
- Enhanced transparency would strengthen accountability journalism and democratic oversight

**Confidence:** 🟢 Low (in a 12-month horizon) — structural improvements are multi-year projects
