### TA-10-2026-0075 — European Semester 2026
**Date Adopted**: 2026-03-11 | **Committee**: ECON | **Status**: ACCESSIBLE

Political context: The European Semester 2026 resolution (adopted March 11, two weeks before the
March 26 session) sets the EU's macro-economic coordination framework for the year. Key implications:
- Confirms Germany's continued excessive deficit procedure obligations
- Sets convergence criteria timelines for potential euro-area candidates
- Provides the fiscal coordination backdrop for BRRD3/SRMR3 implementation
- Links to TA-10-2026-0096 (US tariff response) via economic policy instrument coherence

Cross-reference to TA-10-2026-0091 (BRRD3) and TA-10-2026-0092 (SRMR3): The European Semester
framework is the macro-fiscal context within which Banking Union resolution tools operate. Member
states with excessive deficit procedures have constrained fiscal space to fund potential resolution
fund contributions, making the "minimum harmonization vs maximum protection" debate in SRMR3 more
politically charged.
