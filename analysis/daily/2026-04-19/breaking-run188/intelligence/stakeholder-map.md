# 👥 Stakeholder Map — Run 188

**Focus:** Key actors for March 26 landmark texts and post-recess period

## Tier 1 Stakeholders (Decision-makers)

### EPP Group (~187 seats)
**Position on Banking Union**: Supportive of SRMR3/BRRD3 as German CDU/CSU-aligned positions historically favor Banking Union completion. However, EPP contains Austrian Freedom Party-adjacent members skeptical of deposit guarantee harmonization (DGSD2 federalism concerns).
**Position on Anti-Corruption**: Supportive — EPP has championed Rule of Law conditionality.
**Position on US tariffs**: Divided — German industry MEPs prefer de-escalation; French EPP supports retaliation.

### S&D Group (135 seats)
**Position on Banking Union**: Strongly supportive. S&D has consistently pushed for SRMR3 and deposit guarantee unification since 2015.
**Position on Anti-Corruption**: Champion — Social Democrats drafted key amendments. Most will cite as landmark achievement.
**Position on US tariffs**: Supports proportionate response. Labor/social dimension of US-EU trade relationship important.

### Renew Europe (77 seats)
**Position on Banking Union**: Supportive (French liberal and Nordic MEPs). Some concerns about SRMR3 burden-sharing.
**Position on Anti-Corruption**: Supportive. Transparency and governance are core Renew values.
**Position on US tariffs**: Most hawkish on digital sovereignty dimension — AI Act, DMA defense against USTR is existential for Renew's political identity.

## Tier 2 Stakeholders (Influencers)

### European Central Bank
**Banking Union interest**: HIGH. ECB as SSM supervisor interfaces directly with BRRD3/SRMR3 architecture. ECB will publish implementation guidance once Council ratification complete.

### European Commission (DG FISMA, DG JUST, DG TRADE)
**Multiple texts**: Commission proposed all three Banking Union texts. DG TRADE manages US tariff response. DG JUST leads Anti-Corruption Directive implementation. Post-adoption focus shifts to delegated acts and implementing regulations.

### German Federal Government / Bundesrat
**Banking Union critical**: Germany contributes ~26% of Single Resolution Fund contributions. Bundesrat position on SRMR3 will be the key member state signal. Timeline: Bundesrat may receive Banking Union trilogy for scrutiny April-May 2026.

### European Banking Authority (EBA)
**Technical implementation**: EBA will issue binding technical standards under BRRD3/SRMR3 within 18 months. Technical complexity high — will require extensive consultation with banking sector.

### US Trade Representative (USTR)
**Exogenous actor**: USTR Section 301 decision-maker. Katherine Tai successor (2025 administration) policy direction: protectionist with digital sovereignty dimension. Key variable for entire post-recess EP agenda.

### Civil Society (Transparency International, OLAF, national anti-corruption bodies)
**Anti-Corruption Directive**: Will closely monitor transposition implementation. TI expects binding EU standards to significantly improve enforcement in member states with historically weak frameworks (Hungary, Romania, Bulgaria).
