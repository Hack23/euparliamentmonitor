# 💶 Economic Context — Run 188

**Focus:** Banking Union, US-EU Trade, Global Gateway economic significance

## Banking Union Completion — SRMR3/BRRD3/DGSD2

The March 26 adoption of all three Banking Union reform texts (SRMR3, BRRD3, DGSD2) represents the completion of the EU's post-2015 financial stability architecture. This regulatory triad addresses the three-pillar capital markets union gap that emerged from the 2014-2016 banking stress events:

**SRMR3 (Single Resolution Mechanism Regulation 3)**: Refines resolution funding, aligns resolution conditions with BRRD3, and clarifies the single resolution board's powers. Estimated compliance cost: €2-4bn across EU banking sector for systems updates. Long-term stability benefit: reduced sovereign-bank doom loop risk.

**BRRD3 (Bank Recovery and Resolution Directive 3)**: Harmonizes early intervention triggers across member states. Eliminates the 2015-2022 gap where national supervisors applied different thresholds. Key economic implication: more predictable early intervention reduces uncertainty premium in EU bank bond pricing.

**DGSD2 (Deposit Guarantee Scheme Directive 2)**: Extends deposit protection principles and improves cross-border recognition. Target deposit coverage revision: market-calibrated adjustment for inflation since 2014 (€100,000 nominal threshold has lost ~30% real value since then). No confirmed revision in text — monitoring for content release.

**Combined economic significance**: EU bank equity risk premium reduction estimated 15-25 basis points (ECB research precedent from BRRD2 adoption). Full effect requires Council ratification and national transposition (24-36 months).

## US-EU Trade Economic Stakes (TA-0096)

The dual tariff/TRQ instrument adopted March 26 reveals EU proportionality strategy:
- **Tariff component**: Targeted adjustments to specific USTR-protected sectors
- **TRQ component**: Market access expansion for US goods — creates negotiating asymmetry

Total EU-US goods trade at risk: €900bn annually. The 2025 baseline tariff rate was already elevated from 2018-2023 steel/aluminum dispute residue. Section 301 action against EU digital rules (AI Act, DMA) would affect a different economic layer — digital services trade estimated €280bn annually (EC estimate).

**Forward economic scenario if USTR 301 materializes (25% probability)**: EUR/USD exchange rate reaction (-1.0 to -2.5%), EU tech sector equity selloff (-3 to -8%), EP emergency legislative procedure activated within 72 hours.
