🟡 **DG COMP Staff Announcement:** If Commission announces DMA enforcement team expansion

   quarterly trade barrier report, indicates escalating diplomatic tension that could
   stay Commission's hand.

### Chat Control Track (Monitoring: 6-8 week horizon)

   remains internally.

### Budget 2027 Track (Monitoring: 3-4 month horizon)

🟢 **Council Preliminary Mandate (September 2026):** Council will establish its opening
   budget position for 2027. This is the decisive signal for whether trilogue is cooperative
   or adversarial. Watch for Council position on defense spending instrument.

🟢 **Commission Draft Budget (June 2026):** Commission's own draft 2027 budget will
   triangulate between EP guidelines and Council constraints. Any prioritization of Draghi
   agenda items = positive signal for EP's budget strategy.
