## Scoring Methodology

Each SWOT item is scored on two dimensions:
- **Magnitude** (1–10): How significant is the factor?
- **Confidence** (1–10): How confident are we in this assessment?
- **Weighted Score** = Magnitude × (Confidence / 10)
