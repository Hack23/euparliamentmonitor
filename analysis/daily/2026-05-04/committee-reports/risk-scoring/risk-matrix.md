# Risk Matrix — EP Committee Reports Week 27 Apr–4 May 2026

**Article Type:** committee-reports | **Date:** 2026-05-04
**Framework:** 5×5 Probability × Impact Risk Matrix

---

## Risk Matrix Grid

Using standard 5×5 risk matrix:
- **Probability:** 1=Rare (<5%), 2=Unlikely (5-20%), 3=Possible (20-40%), 4=Likely (40-60%), 5=Almost certain (>60%)
- **Impact:** 1=Negligible, 2=Minor, 3=Moderate, 4=Major, 5=Catastrophic
- **Risk score = P × I (1–25)**
