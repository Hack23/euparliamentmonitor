## Situation Assessment

The European Parliament plenary of 19–23 May 2026 concluded the most consequential single-session trade and security legislative package in the EP10 parliamentary term. **Fourteen motions and resolutions were adopted**, including five economic security instruments:

1. **TA-10-2026-0170** — Steel overcapacity protection instrument → triggers Commission anti-subsidy review against Chinese/Indian overcapacity affecting 327,000+ EU steel workers
2. **TA-10-2026-0171** — FDI screening extension (binding) → expands 2019/452 Regulation scope to critical infrastructure, advanced technology, media, food supply chains; binding co-decision
