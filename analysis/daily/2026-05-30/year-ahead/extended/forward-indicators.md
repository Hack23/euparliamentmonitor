## Framework

Forward indicators are observable leading signals whose movement past a defined **trigger threshold** would
change a year-ahead judgement. Each indicator below carries: a **current reading**, a **trigger threshold**,
the **WEP probability band** that the trigger fires within the horizon, and the **signal** — what crossing the
threshold would tell us. Probability bands use Words of Estimative Probability (Almost Certain / Highly Likely
/ Likely / Roughly Even / Even Chance / Unlikely / Highly Unlikely / Almost No Chance). This dashboard is the
monitoring backbone for the year; it exists so that the central forecast can be *revised on evidence* rather
than re-argued from scratch.

Because this is a `degraded-feeds` run (forward EP feeds returned 404; landscape generator timed out), several
"current readings" are structural inferences rather than live feed values, and are flagged accordingly. The
economic indicators are anchored on the A1 live IMF WEO probe (vintage 2025-09-23).

## A. Economic Indicators (IMF-anchored, A1)

### A1 — German real GDP growth trajectory
- **Current reading:** IMF projects **0.79% in 2026**, rising to **1.18% in 2027** — a fragile sub-1% recovery.
- **Trigger threshold:** A downward revision below **0.3% for 2026** (i.e. toward stagnation/recession).
- **WEP band that trigger fires this horizon:** **Unlikely** (the IMF path is modestly upward).
- **Signal:** Recession risk would harden German net-contributor resistance in MFF talks and shrink defence
  headroom. 🟡 MEDIUM.

### A2 — French fiscal deficit
- **Current reading:** IMF puts the French general-government deficit at **-4.94% of GDP in 2026**, easing
  only to **-4.79% in 2027**.
- **Trigger threshold:** Deficit **worsening beyond -5.5% of GDP** or a formal excessive-deficit escalation.
- **WEP band:** **Roughly Even** that fiscal-rule friction intensifies within the horizon.
- **Signal:** Deeper French deficits weaken Paris's capacity to underwrite new EU spending and sharpen the
  MFF north–south split. 🟡 MEDIUM.

### A3 — Italian inflation vs growth gap
- **Current reading:** IMF shows Italian inflation of **2.64% in 2026** against weak growth of **0.52% in 2026**.
- **Trigger threshold:** Inflation **above 3.0%** while growth **below 0.4%** (stagflation signature).
- **WEP band:** **Unlikely** on current IMF path, but monitored.
- **Signal:** Italian stagflation would amplify Southern demands for MFF flexibility and strain ECB-EP
  appointment politics. 🟡 MEDIUM.

### A4 — Aggregate big-three deficit drift
- **Current reading:** All three run deficits above 3% of GDP in 2026 except Italy (-2.82%); Germany
  **-3.78%**, France **-4.94%** (IMF).
- **Trigger threshold:** Two of the three deficits **widening year-on-year into 2027** (IMF projects German
  widening to -4.23%).
- **WEP band:** **Likely** — IMF already projects German and (marginally) French paths consistent with strain.
- **Signal:** Confirms the "fiscal-discipline" media frame's structural footing and a constrained MFF ceiling.
  🟢 HIGH on the data, 🟡 MEDIUM on the political read.

## Calibration Notes

- Probability bands are **horizon-bounded** (within 365 days); the same trigger may be Almost Certain over
  five years yet Unlikely within twelve months — the MFF headline agreement is the clearest example.
- Bands are derived from reference-class base rates plus the live evidence (51 adopted texts, IMF WEO), not
  from roll-call behaviour, which was unavailable this run.
- Thresholds are analytical guidance, not bright lines; a single indicator rarely flips a judgement alone —
  watch for **clusters** (e.g. A4 + B5 + C11 firing together would strongly confirm the right-leaning
  fiscal-attrition scenario).
