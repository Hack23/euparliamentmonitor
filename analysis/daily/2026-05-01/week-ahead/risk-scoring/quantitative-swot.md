# Quantitative SWOT — Week Ahead 4–8 May 2026

**Analysis Date:** 2026-05-01
**Article Type:** week-ahead
**Framework:** Weighted SWOT with Numerical Scoring

---

## Scoring Methodology

Each SWOT factor is scored on two dimensions:
- **Magnitude (1-5):** How large is this factor's potential effect on legislative outcomes?
- **Confidence (1-3):** How certain are we of this factor? (1=speculative, 2=probable, 3=confirmed)
- **Weighted Score = Magnitude × Confidence**

Factors scoring ≥ 9 are Tier 1 strategic factors requiring direct attention.
