# Scenario Forecast — EP Breaking: DMA Enforcement & Ukraine Accountability
**Date:** 2026-05-01 | **Article Type:** breaking | **Confidence:** 🟢 HIGH

## Forecasting Framework

This analysis applies structured probabilistic forecasting (ODNI-compatible) to identify plausible futures following the EP's April 28–30, 2026 session. Four scenario families are constructed using the Futures Wheel methodology and validated against EP institutional history.

**Confidence scales:** 🟢 HIGH (>75% probability), 🟡 MEDIUM (40–75%), 🔴 LOW (<40%)

## Scenario Family 1: DMA Enforcement Trajectory (12-month horizon)

### S1A — Accelerated Enforcement (🟡 55% probability)

**Trigger:** EP resolution + Commission's own enforcement timetable create political pressure for visible enforcement action before end of 2026.

**Sequence of events:**
1. Q2 2026: Commission DG COMP issues formal non-compliance finding against at least one gatekeeper (most likely: Apple App Store iOS restrictions)
2. Q3 2026: Commission opens formal proceedings on Alphabet self-preferencing in EU advertising markets
3. Q4 2026: First DMA fine issued (potentially €3–5bn range — precedent-setting but below maximum)
4. 2027: Compliance behaviours change across major gatekeepers; EU digital single market access improves measurably

**Beneficiaries:** EU consumers, EU digital SMEs, competitors to Big Tech platforms
**Losers:** Apple, Google, Meta share value (regulatory risk premium increases)
**EP role:** EP enforcement demand credited as catalyst; strengthens EP's role as DMA accountability body

**Indicators to monitor:**
- Commission DG COMP press releases on DMA proceedings (May–August 2026)
- CJEU rulings on Apple/Google gatekeeper designation challenges
- EP IMCO committee hearings with Commissioner for Competition

### S1B — Delayed Enforcement / Diplomatic Modulation (🟡 35% probability)

**Trigger:** US diplomatic pressure on EU institutions leads Commission to delay high-profile enforcement actions against US tech companies.

**Sequence of events:**
1. Q2 2026: EU-US Trade and Technology Council (TTC) discussions delay DMA enforcement schedule for US-headquartered gatekeepers
2. Selective enforcement: Commission prioritises Chinese tech (ByteDance/TikTok) over US gatekeepers
3. EP IMCO committee tables critical resolution; EP-Commission tension increases
4. 2027: DMA enforcement perceived as ineffective; creates political capital for far-right "EU overreach" narrative

**Beneficiaries:** US tech companies (short-term); EU-US diplomatic relationship
**Losers:** EP credibility; EU consumers; DMA rule-of-law principle
**EP role:** EP's enforcement demand undermined; creates cross-party frustration

### S1C — CJEU Legal Challenge Stalls Enforcement (🔴 10% probability)

**Trigger:** Apple or Alphabet wins interim injunction at CJEU suspending DMA gatekeeper designation.

**Sequence of events:**
1. CJEU grants interim measures suspending one gatekeeper's obligations
2. Legal uncertainty freezes all enforcement proceedings for 12–18 months
3. EP calls emergency resolution; IMCO launches hearings on DMA legal architecture
4. Legislative proposal for DMA 2.0 tabled to address CJEU concerns

**EP response:** Emergency IMCO hearings; potential DMA revision process

## Scenario Family 2: Ukraine Accountability & Asset Seizure (12-month horizon)

### S2A — Incremental Progress (🟢 60% probability)

**Trigger:** EP resolution + ongoing trilogue on Asset Recovery and Restitution Regulation produces compromise text accepted by Council QMV.

**Sequence of events:**
1. Q2 2026: Trilogue concludes on Asset Recovery Regulation with compromise on private vs. sovereign asset distinction
2. Windfall profits transfers continue (€3.5bn+ annually from frozen Russian assets already in motion)
3. Q3 2026: CJEU issues advisory opinion clarifying legal basis for principal confiscation
4. Q4 2026: Limited confiscation of sovereign assets (state-owned enterprise holdings) begins in Belgium
5. 2027: Ukraine Reconstruction Conference references EP framework as accountability benchmark

**EP role:** Resolution credited as political mandate for Council QMV majority; EP AFET committee monitors implementation

### S2B — Hungary Veto Blocks Council (🟡 30% probability)

**Trigger:** Hungary's veto power in Council on asset recovery blocks all progress on principal confiscation; windfall profits transfers continue by QMV.

**Sequence of events:**
1. Q2 2026: Hungary vetoes Council's Asset Recovery Regulation — blocks unanimity requirement
2. EU considers enhanced cooperation mechanism (Treaty Article 20) for 26 member states
3. Q3 2026: Enhanced cooperation with 26 states proceeds; Hungary excluded from mechanism
4. Asset seizure legally fragmented: some member states proceed bilaterally, others await comprehensive framework

**EP role:** EP resolution remains relevant as political framework but fragmented implementation; EP AFET drafts critical resolution on Hungary's blocking role

### S2C — Russia Escalation Triggers Crisis (🔴 10% probability)

**Trigger:** Russia responds to EP Ukraine accountability resolution with asymmetric action (cyber/hybrid) against EU institutions.

**Sequence of events:**
1. Russia-linked cyber operations target European Parliament networks (precedent: 2023 DDoS attack by Killnet)
2. Potential targeting of Euroclear or Belgian financial infrastructure
3. EP activates crisis communications; EC triggers NIS2 Article 32 coordinated response
4. EP Ukraine resolution reaffirmed with expanded cybersecurity language added

## Scenario Family 3: EU Political Dynamics (6-month horizon)

### S3A — Continued Centre Coalition Holds (🟢 70% probability)

**Scenario:** EPP + S&D + Renew + Greens centre coalition maintains majority on core EU values (digital governance, Ukraine solidarity) through June 2026.

**Drivers:**
- EPP leadership's "Strategic Autonomy" framing aligns with digital enforcement and Ukraine solidarity
- S&D and Renew have structural interest in opposing far-right on EU values agenda
- Greens' 53 seats pivotal for sustainability linkages

**EP legislative output for Q2–Q3 2026:**
- DMA enforcement accountability resolution (follow-up to April 30)
- Platform Work Directive final trilogues
- EU Chips Act 2.0 preparatory resolution
- Ukraine Reconstruction Financing Instrument

### S3B — Right-Ward Drift Challenges Coalition (🟡 25% probability)

**Scenario:** Budget pressures (defence vs. climate vs. cohesion) fracture centre coalition; EPP migrates toward ECR on some issues.

**Drivers:**
- 2027 budget negotiation creates zero-sum competition between priorities
- EPP needs ECR support on budget votes if S&D/Greens oppose defence spending increases
- Summer 2026 national elections in several member states shift domestic political climate

**EP impact:**
- DMA enforcement follow-up may soften with ECR amendments
- Ukraine solidarity maintained but at lower intensity (less hawkish on asset seizure)

### S3C — Far-Right Political Crisis (🔴 5% probability)

**Scenario:** Major corruption scandal or internal split weakens PfE or ECR, reshaping EP right-wing bloc.

**Probability:** Low based on current group stability metrics
**EP impact:** If materialised, would strengthen centre coalition's majority and accelerate EP legislative programme
