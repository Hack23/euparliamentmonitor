## Methodology

Significance scores use a 1–10 composite scale across four dimensions:
- **Immediacy** (0–3): How time-sensitive is the development?
- **Scope** (0–3): How many stakeholders/domains are affected?
- **Novelty** (0–2): Does this represent a new development vs. incremental?
- **Institutional weight** (0–2): Is this legislative (binding) or political (non-binding)?

**Score interpretation:** 9–10 CRITICAL | 7–8 HIGH | 5–6 MEDIUM | 3–4 LOW | 1–2 MINIMAL
