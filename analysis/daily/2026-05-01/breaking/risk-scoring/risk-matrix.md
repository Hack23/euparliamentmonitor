## Risk Assessment Framework

This risk matrix applies the ISO 31000:2018 risk management standard adapted for parliamentary intelligence analysis. Risks are assessed across four dimensions:
- **Likelihood (L):** 1=Rare (<5%), 2=Unlikely (5–25%), 3=Possible (25–50%), 4=Likely (50–75%), 5=Almost Certain (>75%)
- **Impact (I):** 1=Negligible, 2=Minor, 3=Moderate, 4=Major, 5=Catastrophic
- **Risk Score (R) = L × I:** 1–5 Low, 6–12 Medium, 15–25 High
- **Confidence:** 🟢 HIGH / 🟡 MEDIUM / 🔴 LOW
