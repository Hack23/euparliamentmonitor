## Key Intelligence Findings

### Finding 1 — AI Trade Strategy: From Competitiveness Compass to Parliamentary Mandate

The EP adopted TA-10-2026-0183, the INI resolution on *"Opportunities and challenges presented by a comprehensive artificial intelligence strategy for EU trade"*, following a debate on May 19 and vote on May 20. Rapporteur: INTA committee (enrichment incomplete — rapporteur identity not returned by EP API). The resolution:

- Calls on the Commission to integrate AI into the **EU Digital Trade Strategy**, expected Q3 2026
- Positions the EU as a rules-based AI-trade standard-setter against US and Chinese unilateralism
- Endorses AI-enabled customs modernisation, supply-chain transparency, and SME digital onboarding programmes
- Cross-references the EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689) and the Digital Markets Act enforcement action (TA-10-2026-0160, April 30 2026)

**Significance**: INI resolutions carry political, not legal, force. However, the INTA committee's endorsement of an integrated AI-trade doctrine creates a formal parliamentary mandate the Commission cannot easily ignore ahead of its Digital Trade Strategy consultation. The timing — immediately following the US tariff adjustment package (TA-10-2026-0096, March 2026) — signals the EP wants a proactive AI trade agenda, not merely defensive trade-defense tools.

### Finding 2 — Forest Reproductive Material Regulation: SIGNED 20 May 2026

Procedure 2023/0228(COD) completed the full legislative cycle with signature on May 20 2026. The regulation:

- Modernises EU seed-stock rules for forests first established in 2002
- Introduces **climate-provenance tracking** for planting stock, enabling foresters to match seed sources to projected 2050 climate zones
- Establishes a new category of *"climate-tested reproductive material"* enabling cross-region seed movement to support forest adaptation
- Applies from 2028 (transitional provisions for existing stocks)

**Significance**: The regulation represents one of the most technically specific climate-adaptation measures passed in the current EP term, directly operationalising the EU Forest Strategy (2021) and EU Biodiversity Strategy 2030 commitments. The AGRI-ENVI committee alignment signals a new norm of co-ownership between agricultural and environmental committees on biodiversity-adjacent files.

### Finding 3 — Welfare of Dogs and Cats: New EU Consumer-Animal Welfare Framework

TA-10-2026-0115 (procedure 2023/0447(COD)) adopted April 28 2026 establishes:

- Mandatory EU-wide microchip database for dogs and cats by 2028
- Ban on commercial sale of animals without verified breeder registration
- Cross-border traceability requirements targeting illegal puppy mills
- Minimum welfare standards for companion animal trade across all 27 Member States

**Significance**: Estimated to affect 180+ million companion animals in the EU. Strong consumer protection and animal-welfare coalition driven by AGRI-ENVI alignment. Public consultation showed 95%+ citizen support in 2023 Commission impact assessment. Implementation responsibility distributed across Member States with Commission oversight mechanism.

### Finding 4 — Digital Markets Act Enforcement: Parliament Pushes for Stronger Action

TA-10-2026-0160 (*"Enforcement of the Digital Markets Act"*, April 30 2026, procedure 2026-2596) was an urgency resolution calling on the Commission to:

- Accelerate DMA gatekeepers designation reviews for Apple, Meta, and Amazon
- Impose provisional measures against interoperability refusal practices
- Clarify the relationship between DMA and the AI Act for AI-enabled algorithmic gatekeeping

**Significance**: The resolution signals legislative frustration with enforcement pace 2 years after the DMA took effect. Commission DG COMP has opened formal non-compliance proceedings against Google and Apple; this resolution raises the political stakes ahead of the EC's 2026 DMA review assessment.
